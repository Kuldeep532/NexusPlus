# Nexus Plus Premium and Credit Model

The active commercial model is monthly membership first, with optional AI credit top-ups.

## Monthly memberships

- Lifeline: ₹49/month, 100 included AI credits.
- Super: ₹149/month, 350 included AI credits.
- Pro: ₹399/month, 1,000 included AI credits.

These amounts are the current product configuration; operating and provider costs should be reviewed before publishing pricing.

## Top-up rule

Credit top-ups are not a standalone product. A user must have an active Nexus Plus membership before purchasing any top-up.

## AI feature pricing

Every server-backed AI feature should have a single credit_cost in the Supabase feature catalog. Local/on-device features can remain free.

## Administration

Plan catalog, feature costs, user entitlements, payment verification, and administrative credit adjustments remain server-side. The mobile application only displays the resulting entitlement/balance.

## Legacy catalog

Legacy standalone credit plans remain inactive for compatibility and historical data. They are not offered as a separate subscription product.
